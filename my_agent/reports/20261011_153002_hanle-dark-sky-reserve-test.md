# Hanle Dark Sky Reserve

The Hanle Dark Sky Reserve (HDSR) is an approximately 22 km-radius area around Hanle in Ladakh, within the Changthang Wildlife Reserve, planned as India's and South Asia's first dark sky reserve. Hanle has one of India's darkest skies and hosts the Indian Astronomical Observatory (IAO) of the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA, Bengaluru).
