**1. Summary and Key Findings**
The Hanle Dark Sky Reserve (HDSR) is a designated zone in eastern Ladakh, India, characterized by an exceptionally dark night sky. It serves as a dual-purpose initiative combining scientific preservation with community-based astro-tourism. The reserve is centered around the Indian Astronomical Observatory (IAO), which houses the primary telescope. The project is described as science-driven, aiming to balance the protection of astronomical viewing conditions with the socio-economic benefits for local communities through regulated tourism and strict light pollution management.

**2. Verified Facts and Uncertainties**
*   **Verified Fact:** The HDSR was officially notified by the Government of Ladakh in **December 2022**.
*   **Verified Fact:** The reserve is centered on the **Indian Astronomical Observatory (IAO)** in Hanle.
*   **Verified Fact:** The IAO is operated by the **Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA)**, an autonomous institution under the Department of Science and Technology (DST).
*   **Verified Fact:** The project has two main pillars: curtailing light pollution and promoting astro-tourism for local community benefit.
